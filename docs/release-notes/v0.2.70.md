修复版：V4L2 M2M 硬件编码黑屏与音频时间轴漂移。/ Fix release: black screen with V4L2 M2M encoders and audio timeline drift.

### 修复 / Fixed
- **V4L2 M2M 编码器首帧参数集被丢弃导致黑屏（PR #270，感谢 @PhorusYu）**：V4L2 M2M 编码器不重复发送头信息，首个访问单元只有 AUD、SPS、PPS，IDR 在下一个单元；此前该单元因“不含独立帧”被丢弃，参数集随之丢失，控制端一直停在「正在传输画面」。现在不含图像的单元会暂存，并拼到下一个带图像的单元前面。/ V4L2 M2M encoders do not repeat headers, so their first access unit is AUD,SPS,PPS with the IDR in the next unit. That unit used to be dropped as "not independent", losing the parameter sets and leaving the viewer on "transferring". A unit without a picture is now held and prepended to the next unit that carries one.
- **Ogg 页含多个 Opus 包导致音频卡顿后加速（PR #270）**：读取端把每个 Ogg 页当作一个 20 ms 帧，但 ffmpeg 有时在一页里放两个包，媒体时间轴因此落后于实际时间，控制端先积压再加速播放。现在让 ffmpeg 每个包刷一页。/ The reader treated each Ogg page as one 20 ms frame, but ffmpeg sometimes put two packets in a page, so the timeline drifted behind wall clock and the viewer stuttered then rushed. ffmpeg now flushes a page after every packet.

单元测试与 CI；维护方未在 V4L2 M2M 设备上实机验收。/ Unit tests and CI; not field-tested by the maintainer on V4L2 M2M hardware.
