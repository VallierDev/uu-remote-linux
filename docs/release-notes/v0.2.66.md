功能版：被控端虚拟手柄与震动回传、X11 防窥、portal 授权自愈、弱网帧率回升修复。/ Feature release: host-side virtual gamepad with rumble, X11 privacy screen, self-healing portal grants, frame-rate recovery fix on lossy-RTT paths.

### 新增 / Added
- **被控端虚拟手柄（#263，默认开启）**：手机端虚拟手柄与 SDL 控制端转发的实体手柄发来的 `gpd_*` 消息，被注入为内核级 Xbox 360 手柄（最多 4 个，X11/Wayland 均可），游戏的双马达震动按 `gpd_vibration` 回传控制端。需要 /dev/uinput 权限；`UU_GAMEPAD=0` 整体关闭，`UU_GAMEPAD_RUMBLE=0` 只关震动；`doctor` 报告手柄后端状态。/ `gpd_*` messages from the mobile virtual gamepad or SDL-forwarded physical pads are injected as kernel Xbox 360 pads (up to 4, X11 and Wayland); dual-motor rumble is sent back as `gpd_vibration`. Needs /dev/uinput access; `UU_GAMEPAD=0` disables it, `UU_GAMEPAD_RUMBLE=0` disables rumble only; `doctor` reports the backend.
- **X11 防窥（#223）**：Xorg 会话用 xrandr 把各输出 gamma/亮度归零实现关屏，采集画面不受影响；退出、服务停止或崩溃后自动还原。只支持关屏，不禁用本地键鼠。/ On Xorg the privacy screen zeroes each output's gamma/brightness via xrandr while capture stays intact, restored on exit, stop or crash. Screen-off only; local input is not blocked.

### 修复 / Fixed
- **portal 授权自愈（#248）**：restore token 被 portal 拒绝后不再把已失效的 token 写回缓存导致无人值守主机永久无画面；改为丢弃并无 token 重试一次，token 未实际提交时保留缓存授权。/ A rejected restore token is no longer written back (which left unattended hosts permanently without a picture); it is dropped and retried once without a token, and the cached grant is kept when the token was never submitted.
- **防窥自动开启时机（#253）**：等到采集真正出帧后才开启，避免 GNOME 关屏后恢复 ScreenCast 失败、控制端 20 秒视频超时。/ Auto-enable now waits for real capture frames, avoiding a failed GNOME ScreenCast restore and the viewer's 20 s video timeout.
- **帧率自适应回升（#262）**：零丢包、仅 RTT 抖动的链路不再把帧率钉在 5 fps；回升改为逐格爬梯而非一步跳顶。/ Lossless RTT-jitter paths no longer pin the frame rate at 5 fps; recovery climbs one step at a time instead of jumping to the ceiling.
